Why Nawa

Everything You Need to Excel

AI-powered analytics, exam simulation, dual leaderboards, and 5 types of flashcards — one platform for serious medical students.

🧠
Smart Question Bank
12,000+ MCQs across all specialties. Filter by module, block, difficulty, and type (Unused/Incorrect/Bookmarked/Omitted).
📝
Practice (Tutor) Mode
Immediate feedback after each answer with full explanation, key takeaway, and a short video solution for every question.
🎯
Exam Mode
Full timed exam simulation. No hints during the exam. Complete review with all explanations and video solutions after submission.
⚡
Quick Practice
Fast daily 10-question sessions. Perfect for keeping your streak alive. Minimal interface, maximum focus.
🤖
AI Test Analysis
AI analyzes ALL your quizzes, identifies weak spots by System/Subject/Topic, and builds a custom Study Plan to boost your score.
📅
AI Study Planner
Set your exam date and available schedule. The AI builds a personalized revision timetable down to the daily level automatically.
🃏
5-Type Flashcards PRO
Per-lecture cards, ready decks, custom cards, AI-generated from text, and AI-generated from your wrong answers. Spaced repetition built in.
📊
Deep Analytics
Track performance by module, block, and topic. Peer Score shows how you compare to colleagues after every quiz. Know exactly where to focus.
🏆
Dual Leaderboard
Global ranking + per-module leaderboards. Fair scoring = Accuracy × Questions. Anti-abuse minimum threshold. Anonymous mode available.
🎖️
Badges & Titles
Earn badges per medical module. Special titles for top performers. Showcase your strongest specialty on your profile.
🔥
Streak Calendar
GitHub-style daily activity calendar. Current and longest streak tracking. Build unstoppable momentum.
📋
Tasks, Notes & Exams
Study tasks with deadlines. Personal notes linked to modules. Upcoming exam countdowns. Organized in one place.
📚
Medical Library
Curated resources, textbooks, and reference materials organized by system and module. Everything in one place.
🤝
Custom Quiz (Co-op)
You and your study buddy answer the same questions in the same order from different devices. Study together, compete together.
🎬
Video Solutions
Short video explanation for every single question. Linked directly to the question — watch instantly after answering.
Compete & Win

Rise to the Top of Your Generation

Global rankings + per-module leaderboards. Fair scoring. Top 3 highlighted. Anonymous mode available.
